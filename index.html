Think Well

Mental Health & Emotions

Why It Matters

  • Your mental health affects your decisions, work, and relationships
  • Managing emotions helps you handle stress better
  • Positive thinking helps your immune system work better
  • Mental strength helps you bounce back from tough times
  • Studies show better mental health improves school and work performance

How to Improve

  • Daily Mindfulness: 10-15 minutes of quiet meditation
  • Challenge Negative Thoughts: Question negative thinking patterns
  • Keep a Journal: Write down thoughts and feelings
  • Talk to Someone: Friend, family member, or counselor
  • Digital Breaks: Take regular breaks from screens and social media
  • Creative Activities: Art, music, or writing to express emotions

Sleep Well

Rest & Recovery

Why It Matters

  • Sleep helps your brain store memories and learn better
  • Good rest helps control your hunger and energy levels
  • During deep sleep, your body repairs itself
  • Not sleeping enough raises your risk for many health problems
  • Quality sleep improves focus and reaction time

How to Improve

  • Regular Schedule: Go to bed and wake up at the same time daily
  • Cool, Dark Room: Keep your bedroom between 65-68°F
  • No Screens Before Bed: Put devices away 1 hour before sleep
  • Bedtime Routine: Read, stretch, or do breathing exercises
  • Watch Caffeine: No coffee or energy drinks after 2pm
  • Morning Light: Get sunlight early in the day

Move Well

Physical Activity & Exercise

Why It Matters

  • Regular movement improves heart health and helps you live longer
  • Exercise releases feel-good chemicals that fight stress and sad feelings
  • Physical activity helps your brain work better and boosts creativity
  • Strength training keeps your muscles strong as you grow
  • Active people have a much lower risk of getting sick

How to Improve

  • Daily Movement: Try for 10,000 steps or 30 minutes of activity
  • Build Strength: Do pushups, squats, or weights 2-3 times weekly
  • Stay Flexible: Regular stretching or yoga
  • Find Fun: Choose activities you enjoy like dancing or sports
  • Take Breaks: Stand up and move during long sitting periods
  • Buddy System: Exercise with friends for motivation

Eat Well

Nutrition & Hydration

Why It Matters

  • Food directly affects your brain function and energy levels
  • Drinking enough water improves focus and physical performance
  • A balanced diet reduces inflammation and disease risk
  • Gut health affects your mood and immune system
  • Good food choices can help you live 10-15 years longer

How to Improve

  • More Plants: Fill half your plate with vegetables and fruits
  • Quality Protein: Include eggs, lean meat, beans, or tofu at meals
  • Stay Hydrated: Drink 8 glasses of water daily
  • Eat Mindfully: Slow down and avoid distractions while eating
  • Plan Ahead: Prepare healthy meals and snacks in advance
  • Eat Colorfully: Different colored foods provide different nutrients

Manage Habits

Controlling Harmful Behaviors

Why It Matters

  • Unhealthy habits can take over your brain's reward system
  • Bad habits can damage relationships and school performance
  • Managing behaviors protects your physical and mental health
  • Unhealthy habits can reduce your energy and focus
  • Phone/social media overuse can hurt sleep quality and concentration

How to Improve

  • Know Your Triggers: Identify what causes unhealthy habits
  • Healthy Swaps: Replace bad habits with good ones
  • Get Support: Talk to friends, family, or join support groups
  • Set Limits: Create clear rules around potentially harmful behaviors
  • Screen Boundaries: Use app timers and phone-free zones
  • Ask for Help: Talk to a counselor for serious concerns
