Think Well

Mental Health & Emotions

Why It Matters

  • Your mental health affects decisions, work, and relationships
  • Managing emotions lowers stress
  • Positive thinking supports immunity
  • Mental strength builds resilience
  • Better mental health boosts performance

How to Improve

  • Daily Mindfulness: 10–15 minutes of meditation
  • Challenge Negative Thoughts: reframe them
  • Keep a Journal: write feelings
  • Talk to Someone: friend, family, counselor
  • Digital Breaks: regular screen-free time
  • Creative Activities: art, music, writing

Sleep Well

Rest & Recovery

Why It Matters

  • Sleep consolidates memory and learning
  • Good rest balances hunger hormones
  • Body repair happens in deep sleep
  • Sleep debt raises health risks
  • Quality sleep sharpens focus

How to Improve

  • Regular Schedule: same bedtime/wakeup
  • Cool, Dark Room: 18 °C (65 °F)
  • No Screens: 1 h before bed
  • Bedtime Routine: read, stretch
  • Limit Caffeine: none after 2 p.m.
  • Morning Light: daylight soon after waking

Move Well

Physical Activity & Exercise

Why It Matters

  • Movement strengthens heart and longevity
  • Exercise releases mood-boosting endorphins
  • Activity sparks creativity and brain health
  • Strength training preserves muscle
  • Active people have lower disease risk

How to Improve

  • Daily Movement: 10 000 steps or 30 min
  • Build Strength: push-ups, squats 2–3×/wk
  • Stay Flexible: stretch or yoga
  • Find Fun: dance, sports, games
  • Take Breaks: stand every hour
  • Buddy System: exercise with friends

Eat Well

Nutrition & Hydration

Why It Matters

  • Food powers brain and energy
  • Hydration boosts focus
  • Balanced diet reduces inflammation
  • Gut health shapes mood and immunity
  • Good food adds 10–15 years of life

How to Improve

  • More Plants: half your plate
  • Quality Protein: eggs, beans, tofu
  • Stay Hydrated: 8 glasses water
  • Eat Mindfully: slow, no screens
  • Plan Ahead: meal-prep snacks
  • Eat Colorfully: rainbow produce

Manage Habits

Controlling Harmful Behaviors

Why It Matters

  • Unhealthy habits hijack reward systems
  • They strain relationships and work
  • Balanced habits protect health
  • Poor habits drain energy
  • Overuse of tech impairs sleep

How to Improve

  • Know Triggers: identify cues
  • Healthy Swaps: replace bad routines
  • Get Support: friends or groups
  • Set Limits: clear boundaries
  • Screen Boundaries: timers, phone-free zones
  • Ask for Help: counselor if needed
